- The driven lookup grows with its targets and is chunked by the parameter budget. EXECUTED, `r02`: one statement of 32,700 binds is accepted by both engines. Its cost on Postgres is not held to its targets as written: M1.

## Premortem

The spec was built as written and failed. The three most likely reasons:

1. The first landing's gate went red on every test that writes at a deleted path, and the generation was added to the write's columns in the build with no sentence of the spec behind it. This is H1.
2. A mount ran its first `destroy` at a path, and from then on every rebuild reported a repaired edge that the next rebuild removed; a file restored into a directory made again was missing from every walk of the fs edges. This is H2.
3. The Postgres leg's count test of the driven lookup failed, or passed on a small table and a restore of a large batch read the whole entries table in production. This is M1.

# Study: Sessions narrowing, sub-sessions, the subject set's lifecycle, and the pending kind

## Verdict

Live, in two parts that land at different gates. Nothing of spec 148 is in the tree: `Narrowing` still has the one member `NONE`, the session has no `narrow`, `child`, `join` or `answer`, and `ErrorKind` has no pending kind (EXECUTED, an interpreter probe; READ, `src/vfs/authority.py:65-68`).

Section 5 (`pending` and the ask ledger) is placed at gate b as roadmap story H3, "spec 148 §5 rebuilt on H1 and K5", so its conversion is a rewrite onto the H0/H1/H2/K5 design: a host policy hook, a ticket, `fs.answer` as a management method and a re-send under `request_key=` (READ, `context/standards/roadmap.md:308-318`).

Sections 1 to 4 (narrowing with content, child sessions, join) are deferred to 0.2 by the roadmap's not-in-0.1.0 table (READ, `context/standards/roadmap.md:1051`). Section 6 (a per-session namespace overlay) is a conditional ("if it returns") with no roadmap story; it folds into the 0.2 narrowing unit as the subtractive-only rule (INFERRED).

Verdict value chosen: live-0.1.0, because the H3 slice is gate b; the remainder is live-0.2 (INFERRED).

## Units proposed

1. Slug: a policy hook can stop a kernel verb into a pending result that an attributed answer admits once. Title: "A call a policy hook or a liftable refusal stops returns a pending result with a ticket, and an attributed answer lets the identical call through exactly once." Gate b. Depends on H0 (the action log, the ledger's home), H1 (the interrupt rule, carried by a spec), H2 (`plan=`, the approver's preview), K5 (spec 177's claim-first replay row and `request_key`), D9, D10. Source: spec 148 §5 and its open question on the ledger's home; roadmap H3 criteria 1-8. Rewrite: the roadmap's design (hook before storage, ledger consulted ahead of the hook, ticket equal to run id for a bare verb, `fs.answer` on the router with `preview=`, replay hit on the second re-send, `sweep` reclaims past TTL) replaces 148's digest-keyed ledger and grant-channel answer.
2. Slug: a session narrows by a subtractive profile and a profile refusal is classified apart from a grant refusal. Title: "`session.narrow(profile)` returns a session that can do no more than before, hides paths before any lookup, and refuses a denied verb with a kind distinct from a grant refusal; a child session inherits the parent's narrowing, subjects and source identity and ends with its parent." Gate 0.2. Depends on spec 070 and 058 (landed), the H1 spec only for the kind table, and the 0.2 opening of the frozen surface (the freeze list K9 forbids changing `Narrowing`'s meaning after b1 without an addition being listed). Source: spec 148 §1-3 and §6. Amend: the content stands; ADR citations, the refusal kinds and the resolver rule change (below).
3. Slug: a joiner narrows the next call and is told which surfaced paths it cannot see. Title: "`session.join(principal)` narrows the very next call to the new subject set, re-grades nothing already surfaced, and returns the surfaced paths the joiner cannot read." Gate 0.2. Depends on unit 2, spec 167 §7 (the `history` ring whose entries carry `surfaced`) and, if the leak notice also covers verb calls outside `cli`, K10's stamp table. Source: spec 148 §4. Amend.

## Open choices, each verified

1. The refusal when a profile would widen: `vfs.invalid` or a classified permission kind; lean `invalid`. Verification: holds-amended. Precedent: Landlock's layers only stack and Capsicum's rights only shrink (READ, `context/research/2026-09-05-permissions-lens-unix-lineage.md:23`, `:45`), and the spec's own 2026-10-15 amendment makes the overlay "subtractive only (hide, deny, no session-level bind)" (READ, `context/specifications/active/148-sessions-narrowing-and-pending/spec.md:4`). Amended text: the profile document holds only subtractive rules (hide globs, verb denies, per-prefix narrowing), with no show globs, so `narrow` is an intersection that cannot widen by construction and needs no widen refusal; a malformed profile (an empty glob, an unknown verb) is `vfs.invalid`.
2. Profile rule resolution: Mirage's anchor depth or vfs's longest prefix; lean longest prefix. Verification: holds-amended. Precedent: the Mirage memo states "Longest prefix *is* anchor depth for exact paths; Mirage's contribution is the extension to patterns" (READ, `context/research/2026-09-04-mirage-design-patterns.md:96-98`); `PermissionMap` is longest-prefix (READ, `src/vfs/permissions.py:23`). Amended text: one rule, anchor depth (literal components before the first wildcard), which equals longest prefix on exact paths; at equal depth a deny wins. A profile with globs cannot be ranked by longest prefix alone (INFERRED).
3. The profile refusal at verb time is a sub-kind apart from the grant refusal; lean a sub-kind (the spec names `vfs.permission_denied.profile`). Verification: holds. Precedent: Capsicum's distinct `ENOTCAPABLE` versus `EACCES`, marked "take" (READ, `context/research/2026-09-05-permissions-lens-unix-lineage.md:83`, `:171`); child kinds that degrade to a known parent are the tree's pattern (READ, `src/vfs/results/kinds.py:31-42`, `vfs.budget_exhausted.authority`). A hidden path answers as absent, not as a refusal, per the spec's projection-before-lookup rule (READ, spec.md:18) and the landed rule that a miss beneath an unreadable row answers as a miss (READ, `context/specifications/active/2026-11-14-a-miss-beneath-a-row-the-caller-cannot-read-answers-as-a-miss-beneath-nothing.md`, title).
4. A child may add subjects but never remove one; lean yes, adding is narrowing. Verification: holds-amended. Precedent: under the intersection law a larger set has a smaller meet; macaroon caveats only accumulate (READ, `context/research/2026-09-05-permissions-lens-capabilities-and-delegation.md:45`); AWS chained sessions cannot outlive the parent or change `SourceIdentity`, marked "take" (READ, same memo `:199`, `:210`). Amended text: a child may add subjects up to `MAX_SUBJECTS` (READ, `src/vfs/authority.py:60`); a set past the bound is `vfs.budget_exhausted.authority`; removal is refused `vfs.invalid`.
5. The leak notice and its memory: in scope here or later; lean a `surfaced` ring buffer with a declared cap, here. Verification: already-ruled for the buffer: the 2026-10-15 amendment says "spec 148's ring is that field, not a second buffer" (READ, spec.md:4), and spec 167 §7 declares it as `history` entries of at most 256 surfaced paths in a ring of 1,000 entries (READ, `context/specifications/active/167-the-shell-pipeline-core/spec.md:168`). The scope is ruled by the roadmap: join is part of §1-4, 0.2 (READ, `context/standards/roadmap.md:1051`). Needs a ruling at conversion: whether paths surfaced by direct verb calls (not `cli`) count, read from K10's stamp table (READ, `context/standards/roadmap.md:213-223`), or the notice covers shell history only.
6. Join re-grades nothing already surfaced. Decided 2026-09-06 (READ, spec.md:21). Already-ruled. Prior art runs the other way for reading history (the joiner widens) and the memo marks it "take as an open decision" (READ, `context/research/2026-09-05-permissions-lens-agent-native-2026.md:225`, `:252`); the spec's dated ruling closes it.
7. Where the ask ledger lives: in storage per mount, or on the router, one per instance. Verification: needs-a-ruling on the schema, the router-memory option fails. A ledger held in process memory fails H3 criterion 6 (a resume from a second `DatabaseStorage` succeeds) and H1's "a resume can arrive at a different process" (READ, `context/standards/roadmap.md:295`, `:316`). Between the storage options the roadmap holds two leans: D10 "the mount's schema" and H3 criterion 6 "the mount's schema" (READ, `:1082`, `:316`) against H1 criterion 5 and H4 rule iv "the root binding's schema" (READ, `:295`, `:322`). Needs a ruling: the ledger in the mount's schema of the ticket's path, or in the root binding's schema beside the action log and the replay store.
8. Kind shape of the suspended step (D10): top-level `vfs.pending` with retry class `resume`, or a child of `permission_denied`; lean top-level. Verification: holds; precedent none found in landed code; rests on the longest-prefix dispatch rule (READ, `src/vfs/results/kinds.py:31-42`): a child of `permission_denied` would land in old consumers as a final refusal, and a suspended step is resumable.
9. Whether vfs carries `pending` at all (D9, amending ADR 084 pin 4); lean yes, vfs never asks and never blocks. Verification: holds. Precedent: the MCP mapping of `pending` to `input_required` planned in M3 and §11's goal (READ, `context/standards/roadmap.md:757`, `:789`); durable-workflow resumption cited in the domain introduction (READ, `:247`), stated there as unverified knowledge. The spec's own rule "storage never blocks, crashes or auto-rejects" is the same line (READ, spec.md:22).
10. The pending TTL; lean 24 hours (D10). Verification: holds. Precedent: Decided 2026-11-15: a stored first answer is kept 24 hours by default (READ, `context/specifications/active/2026-11-15-the-kernels-public-names-and-argument-families-are-ruled.md:83`; roadmap D11 at `:1083`).
11. The per-session namespace overlay "if it returns" (§6). Verification: holds, folded into unit 2 as the subtractive rule. Precedent: Plan 9's `RFNOMNT` named in the spec and Capsicum's set-only capability mode (READ, `context/research/2026-09-05-permissions-lens-unix-lineage.md:45`); a mount-adding overlay has no roadmap story (EXECUTED, grep of the roadmap).
12. A closed session's refusal kind (raised by spec 070 for 148). Verification: holds as built (`vfs.invalid`); precedent the landed code (READ, `src/vfs/session.py:41-47`). No 148 unit needs to change it.

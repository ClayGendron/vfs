# Study: Binary bodies and text renderings: bytes in a blob row, a stamped Markdown rendering in the content row

## Verdict

Partly landed. Slices A (bytes in and out) and B (the renderer seam, the four document renderers and the render stage) are in the tree and have since been hardened by four landed fixes; slice C (the image renderer) and slice D (the documentation) are not built, and two promises of slice B remain unmet: an unchanged bytes overwrite still re-renders, and `search` reports no count of unrendered rows.

The A/B half is archived with a landing note under roadmap story R7 (READ, context/standards/roadmap.md:965: "152 (A/B closed; C/D re-cut or tagged 0.2)"). The C half is live at 0.2 (READ, roadmap.md:582 and :1052). The D half and the two unmet promises are live but not placed by their own story.

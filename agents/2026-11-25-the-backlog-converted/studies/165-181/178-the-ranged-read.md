## Where the roadmap places it

- Story K6, "The ranged read · spec 178 · S · gate b", depends on K3 (READ, `roadmap.md:178-184`). The state table lists `read(range=)` as missing (READ, `roadmap.md:122`) and theme 1 records that no `range=` exists under `src/vfs` (READ, `roadmap.md:94`).
- K6 acceptance 1 asks that `read(path, range=(0, 4096))` on a 4 MB body "binds and fetches at most 4,096 body bytes" and acceptance 4 that a 10,000-path ranged read "binds at most `10,000 × length` body bytes" (READ, `roadmap.md:181`, `:184`). The spec's text window fetches up to `length + 6` bytes (spec §4.3, §5); the two disagree by six bytes a row on a text body (INFERRED). See the open choices.
- The one-line roadmap summary names "the range" among what must land for 0.1.0 (READ, `roadmap.md` section 17).

## Open choices, each verified

1. Spec §7 `[NEEDS CLARIFICATION]` (READ, `spec.md:192`): serve a partial slice unverified (lean), or hash the whole body inside the engine on every ranged read and ship a boolean; spec 175's rebase note adds a third option, per-page hashes stored beside the body (READ, `175-.../spec.md:448`). Not closed by a ruling: D8 fixes the kind and D59 adds an opt-in salvage `read(verify=False)` (READ, `roadmap.md` section 16, rows D8 and D59), neither says what a slice does. Precedent for the lean: Amazon S3 Glacier serves a range that is not tree-hash aligned with no checksum, and returns checksums only for aligned ranges (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/en_us/amazonglacier/latest/dev/checksum-calculations-range.html); that is also precedent for the per-page option. Against it: K3's story is that `read` tells the caller when a body no longer matches its hash (READ, `roadmap.md:154`), and the spec itself records the rule "verify on every read" (READ, `spec.md:186`). Verification: needs-a-ruling. Needs a ruling: a partial slice served unverified with a note, or the whole body hashed in the engine on every ranged read, or per-page hashes (a schema change).
2. The argument's name, `range` over `byte_range` (lean `range`, READ `spec.md:29`). Consistent with roadmap K6, which writes `range=` (READ, `roadmap.md:181`), and with HTTP `Range` (RFC 9110 section 14) and opendal's `read(range)` as cited in the kernel memo. Ruff's `A` rule set is selected (READ, `pyproject.toml:115`), so the ignorelist line is required. Verification: holds.
3. The unit is bytes for both bodies (lean, `spec.md:35-55`). Consistent with `size_bytes` and `content_hash` being measured on UTF-8 bytes (READ, `src/vfs/models/version.py:71`) and with POSIX `pread`. Verification: holds; precedent POSIX `pread(2)` and HTTP byte ranges.
4. Snap outward to whole characters rather than inward or refuse (lean, `spec.md:61-72`). No engine or standard found that serves a UTF-8 byte range snapped to characters. Verification: holds; precedent none found; rests on the rule that a served text body always decodes (spec §2.3) and on POSIX `pread` returning empty only at end of file.
5. One range per call; per-path and suffix ranges are follow-ups (lean, `spec.md:171-172`). K6 acceptance 4 leaves the batch form to the spec (READ, `roadmap.md:184`). Verification: holds; precedent POSIX `pread`, one offset per call.
6. The K6 byte bound against the six-byte text window: K6 says at most `length` bytes fetched, the spec at most `length + 6` on `content` (READ, `roadmap.md:181`, `:184`; `spec.md:170`). Verification: holds-amended. Amended text for K6 criteria 1 and 4: "fetches at most `length` body bytes from a bytes body and at most `length + 6` from a text body", with the six bytes the outward snap's window; the conversion carries the spec's bound and names the roadmap edit.
7. Oracle's slice as an inline `WITH FUNCTION` declaration, with schema-created functions as the fallback if the leg refuses (lean, `spec.md:134-164`). Oracle documents PL/SQL functions in the `WITH` clause from 12c (Oracle SQL Language Reference, SELECT, `plsql_declarations`). The fallback copies the `after_create` DDL pattern already in the tree (READ, `rows.py:920-926`). Verification: holds; the fork is settled by task 1 on the Oracle leg, an experiment and not a product choice.
8. `read_range` as a one-valued trait (`"engine"`) declared when `body_slice` is set, with a `MountMeta` trait snapshot (lean, `spec.md:178-179`), against spec 179's rebase that makes it a feature (READ, `179-.../spec.md:141`). The tree already carries a one-valued trait, `version_encoding` (READ, `protocol.py:88`), and no `features()` exists; K7 (gate f) introduces it after K6 (gate b) (READ, `roadmap.md:193`). Verification: holds; precedent `version_encoding`; the conversion states that K7 moves it to a feature.
9. The range ceiling at `INT_CEILING`, tied to the `Integer` width of `size_bytes` (lean, `spec.md:31`). AGENTS.md admits a cap an external system imposes; the column type and the engines' value caps are that (READ, `rows.py:442`). Verification: holds; precedent the same `INT_CEILING` bound on every other integer parameter (READ, `params.py:160`, `:253`).
10. The render marker line on a sliced row (lean, `spec.md:196-204`). Verification: holds; precedent the renderer's existing notes for pending renders (READ, `reads.py:865`), and the `more: after=` line K4 asks for (READ, `roadmap.md:165`).

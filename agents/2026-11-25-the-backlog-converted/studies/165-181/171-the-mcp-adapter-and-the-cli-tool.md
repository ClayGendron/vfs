## Verdict

live-0.1.0. Nothing of the spec is built: there is no `src/vfs/mcp/`, no `serve`, no `AGENT_OPS`, no `cli`, no `mcp` extra and no vendored MCP schema (EXECUTED, `ls src/vfs/mcp`, `grep -rn "AGENT_OPS\|def serve\|def cli\|mcp" src/vfs`, `grep -n mcp pyproject.toml`, all empty). The roadmap places it as story M1, gate b, with its §0 split out as story I2, gate b, and MCP was ruled into 0.1.0: "Decided in part (Clay, 2026-11-15): MCP ships with 0.1.0" (READ, `context/standards/roadmap.md:1074`).

The design stands, but its names, counts and shell language are stale: the verbs were renamed, the posture words were replaced by the ladder, the shell became the whole bash language, and specs 174 to 181 add arguments and a tool. Conversion is three units, mostly amends; the `cli` unit is a rewrite of its language-facing text.

2. **`vfs serve` at v1 (spec decision 7: no).** Roadmap D24 leans "Yes" (READ, `roadmap.md:1096`); not marked Decided. HOLDS: precedent is that MCP clients launch stdio servers by command line (Claude Desktop's `mcpServers` config names a `command` and `args`; the reference servers ship as `npx`/`uvx` commands), and roadmap M2 carries it (READ, `roadmap.md:779-783`). 3. **Sessions on the wire (spec: none; D51 lean: a bounded per-principal stamp table inside `serve()`, process-local).** (READ, `roadmap.md:1123`.) Sense and precedent do not settle it.

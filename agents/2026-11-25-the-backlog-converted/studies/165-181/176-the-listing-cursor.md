# Study: The listing cursor on ls, tree and glob: a path key on Result, keyset pages that read around tombstones, and one merge rule for every fan-out

Labels: EXECUTED means a command ran on 2026-11-25 against the working tree (the checkout's log shows `3166469` on top of `0ed462f`; `3166469` only adds templates under `agents/`); READ means read at the cited `path:line`; INFERRED means reasoning from the two.

## Verdict

live-0.1.0. Nothing of the spec is built: no `after=`, `limit=`, `next_after`, `listing_cursor`, `frontier_page`, `local_after`, `LISTING_PAGE` or `LISTING_FILL_CEILING` exists under `src/vfs` or `tests/` (EXECUTED, grep; the one `next_after` hit is an unrelated `int` field of the index pass's cursor, `src/vfs/storage/backends/database/indexing.py:209`). The problem is still true at HEAD, roadmap story K4 places it at gate b, and two of its three markers are closed by the 2026-11-15 rulings.

The native-page index design of slice B is no longer free-standing: the deletes memo of 2026-11-15 proposes a `generation` column in the unique keys `(parent_id, generation, name)` and `(path, generation)`, which would itself keep tombstones out of a listing's key range (READ, `context/research/2026-11-15-deletes-tombstones-and-a-move-that-keeps-its-identity.md:55`, `:60`, `:65`). That deletes spec is not yet written. Slice B's index choice must be taken together with it.

## Where the roadmap places it

- READ, `context/standards/roadmap.md:160-167`: story K4, "The listing cursor · spec 176 · M · gate b", depends on K1 (D6). Its four acceptance criteria: a 10,000-child `ls` in ten pages with `next_after == rows[-1].path` and at most `limit + 1` rows fetched per page with tombstones excluded by the predicate, including 500 tombstones between children 1,000 and 1,001; `tree` and `glob` on the same key with a three-mount fan-out emitting one key; `|` clears the key, `with_mount` rebases it, the renderer prints `more: after=…` only when set; anonymous `ls(limit=)` under a `list` posture pages, under `none` it is `unauthenticated` at the first page.
- READ, `roadmap.md:75`: K4 is in the gate-b list of domain A. READ, `roadmap.md:995-996`: alpha batch a3 ("Cursor, range, the regroup and the freeze list"), with K4 also feeding a4 (H0).

## Units proposed

1. Slug: the listing cursor contract served by a derived page in the router. Title: "`ls`, `tree` and `glob` take `after=` and `limit=` and return the last row's path as `Result.next_after`, paged correctly across every mount for every backend." Gate b. Depends on K1 (D6, decided). Source: spec §1, §2.7 (the derived page), §3, §4 rows for statements, criteria 1-4, 9, 11, 14, 15; tasks 1-4. Conversion: amend (names, faces, line numbers and the default-page ruling change; the design stands). It lands green on its own because every backend, `DatabaseStorage` included, is served by the router's derived page.
2. Slug: native keyset pages in the database storage that read around tombstones. Title: "On every engine a listing page costs a fixed number of statements and visits at most `limit + 1` live rows, however many deleted rows share its directory." Gate b. Depends on unit 1; on the deletes spec's schema ruling (whether `generation` enters the unique keys, the deletes memo `:55-65`); on spec 174's landing order only through the shared schema bump (INFERRED). Source: §2.1-§2.4, §2.6, §4, §5, criteria 5-8, 10, 12, 13 (slice B half); tasks 5-9. Conversion: amend, and a rewrite of §2.2 if the deletes spec adopts the generation key (the extra `(parent_id, deleted_at, name)` index then has no job; the unique `(parent_id, generation, name)` serves the `ls` page).
3. Slug: a tree under max depth pages on a stored depth column. Title: "A `tree` with `max_depth` visits at most `max_depth × (limit + 1)` rows a page, because each row stores its depth beside its path." Gate unplaced. Depends on unit 2. Source: §2.5, criterion 7 (slice C half), criterion 13 (slice C half); tasks 10-11. Conversion: reshape. Worth a spec: EXECUTED in the spec's appendix A (2026-10-24), `tree("/n", max_depth=1, limit=5)` visited 506 and 605 rows for pages of 5, and the reference deployment's first rule is that a read costs in proportion to what it returns, never to the corpus (READ, `AGENTS.md`, "How to use it", per verb call); with a mean depth of 6 and 15% of directories at depth 8 or more, a shallow `tree` near the root of a 10,000,000-file mount visits the whole subtree (INFERRED). Placement recommended beside K4 at gate b or at gate f; Needs a ruling: gate b with K4, or gate f, or 0.2.

## Open choices, each verified

1. §1.1, the default page (`[NEEDS CLARIFICATION]`). Choice: no kernel default with every front paging (lean), or a kernel default of `LISTING_PAGE` on `ls`, `tree`, `glob` with the more-line on every truncated listing. Verification: needs-a-ruling. The lean is consistent with the production posture (an ETL `ls` of 10,000 rows stays whole; READ, `AGENTS.md`, "Production posture") and with glob's and grep's `max_count` defaulting to `None` (READ, `params.py:253`, `:288`). Against it: the constitution's Article 2 §3 is titled "Bounded output by default" (READ, `constitution.md:88`), and `traverse` already has a kernel default, `TRAVERSE_LIMIT_DEFAULT = 128` (READ, `src/vfs/ops.py:189`). Both answers have landed precedent and the article's body ("MUST accept a limit") is met by either, so this is a product choice. Needs a ruling: (a) no kernel default, the MCP tools, the shell and the guests page at `LISTING_PAGE`; or (b) a kernel default `limit=LISTING_PAGE` on the three verbs, with `limit=None` spelled explicitly by ETL callers.
2. §1.2, the key's shape (`[NEEDS CLARIFICATION]`). Choice: the last row's plain path (lean) or a sealed opaque token. Verification: already-ruled. READ, `context/specifications/active/2026-11-15-the-kernels-public-names-and-argument-families-are-ruled.md:65`, decision 5: "the key is the plain path of a row on the page, never a sealed token"; and `:106`.
3. §1.4, where the key lives, pin 12 (`[NEEDS CLARIFICATION]`). Choice: a declared field of `Result` (lean) or a `(Result, key)` pair. Verification: already-ruled. READ, `roadmap.md:1078` (D6, Decided 2026-11-15) and the kernel rulings spec decision 5 (`:63-65`).
4. §1.4, the key's algebra (lean: enrichment keeps it, set algebra and `merge` drop it, rebase moves it, an overflowing rebase ends the listing loudly). Verification: holds. Precedent: the roadmap's K4 criterion 3 asks exactly "`Result | Result` clears the key; `with_mount` rebases it" (READ, `roadmap.md:166`); the rebase rides the existing `Path.with_mount`/`without_mount` seam (READ, `src/vfs/paths.py:296`, `:320`) and the `vfs.unaddressable` overflow rule already used for rows.
5. §1.2 and §2.3, a key is always an emitted row (the fill rule) so it never names a hidden row. Verification: holds. Precedent: the 2026-11-12 archived spec "the scan tier reveals nothing about rows a caller cannot read" sets the same no-leak rule for another read surface (READ, its title in `context/specifications/archive/`); the fetch-`limit + 1` pattern is recorded as SeaweedFS's in the spec's cited study 06 §3.
6. §2.3, `LISTING_FILL_CEILING = 10_000`, justified as "the same order as grep's candidate budget (ADR 033 §3)". Verification: holds-amended. The cited budget no longer exists as a constant: grep is bounded by the shared wall-time `Deadline` (READ, `src/vfs/deadline.py:36`, and the archived `2026-11-07-bounded-work-has-one-home.md`). Amended text: "The fill loop fetches in doubling batches up to `LISTING_FILL_CEILING = 10_000` rows a statement, a per-statement working-set ceiling and not a cap, and checks the call's shared `Deadline` between batches, ending the page loudly with its key when the wall is reached." Precedent for the doubling batch: none found in vfs; it rests on the rule that no statement grows unboundedly (AGENTS.md, production posture).
7. §1.1, `LISTING_PAGE: Final = 1_000` in `vfs/ops.py` "beside `GRAPH_LIMIT_DEFAULT`". Verification: holds-amended. The neighbour is now `TRAVERSE_LIMIT_DEFAULT` (READ, `src/vfs/ops.py:189`); amended text: "`LISTING_PAGE: Final = 1_000` lives in `vfs/ops.py` beside `TRAVERSE_LIMIT_DEFAULT`."
8. §1.1, `ls` refuses `after`/`limit` beside `observations`, and `glob` refuses `limit` beside `max_count`. Verification: holds. Precedent: the mutual-exclusion `ShapeRule`s already in `RULES` (READ, `params.py:447-452`, glob's `paths`/`observations`); §3.4's arithmetic (one statement per directory per page, 10,000 a page for a 10,000-directory batch) fails the reference deployment's per-batch multiplier (READ, `AGENTS.md`, "Per batch"). Retiring glob's `max_count` stays spec 181's (READ, `context/specifications/active/181-the-public-surface-regrouped-by-kernel-group/spec.md:51`, which already lists `after`, `limit` beside `max_count`).
9. §2.2, the axis decision: composite indexes (a) over sweep-scheduled bounding (b), raw-row budgets (c) and partial indexes (d). Verification: holds-amended. The rejection of (d) on dialect reach agrees with the deletes memo's count of three spellings across five engines (READ, the memo `:56`). The memo's generation key is a fifth option the spec did not weigh, measured on SQLite at 54 steps beside 4,000 tombstones against 63 for a live-only key (READ, the memo `:60`). Amended text: "A listing page seeks on a key in which a directory's live rows are contiguous and in name order: `(parent_id, generation, name)` if the deletes spec puts a generation in the unique keys, otherwise the added `(parent_id, deleted_at, name)`; `tree` and `glob` pages seek on `(generation, path)` or the widened `(deleted_at, path)` likewise." The four server-engine plans remain unmeasured for either form (READ, the memo `:77`; the spec §2.2 marks them INFERRED).
10. §2.2, if a planner prefers the unique index, pin the index as a `DialectProfile` field. Verification: holds. Precedent: AGENTS.md admits a profile field "only for a decision SQLAlchemy takes no position on"; `ranges.py` already pins its range join (spec §2.2, READ there; INFERRED that it still does, not re-read here).
11. §2.6, a per-arm `UNION ALL` for glob pages is named and not built. Verification: holds. Precedent: AGENTS.md, "Never design toward a hard scale cap": acknowledge the suboptimal profile in the docstring and name the future direction.
12. §3.3, a dead mount's key is treated as finished and the listing ends with a warning. Verification: holds. Precedent: `Result.merge_branches`' zero-progress demotion of a dead branch to a warning (READ, `envelope.py:590`).
13. §5, Oracle rows-visited counter falls back to plan-derived access predicates when `V$` views are unreadable. Verification: holds. Precedent: the spec's SQLite row is already plan-derived (EXECUTED in its appendix A); engine counters named are documented engine behaviour (`EXPLAIN ANALYZE`, `Handler_read_*`, `STATISTICS XML`, `ALLSTATS LAST`), not re-checked here.
14. Rebase note for spec 180: `sweep(limit=)` returns `Result.next_after`. Verification: fails. READ, the kernel rulings spec decision 5 (`:65`) and its table (`:107`): a scrub, sweep or versions key does not ride `next_after`; each names its own field at conversion. Decision 3 renames `sweep` to `destroy` from the deletes spec's landing (`:112`). The alternative is determined: drop the 180 note; spec 180's conversion names its own field and may reuse `frontier_page`.
15. §1.3, each page is its own read transaction, with POSIX `readdir`'s guarantee across pages. Verification: holds. Precedent: POSIX `readdir` (a file added or removed during iteration may or may not be returned); `pages.py` fetches each page whole in its own statement (READ, `pages.py:5-9`).

## Notes for the conversion

- Read D6 and the kernel rulings spec's decision 5 as settled: `next_after: Path | None` on `Result`, a plain path of a row on the page.
- Add the roadmap's K4 criterion 4 explicitly: anonymous `ls(limit=)` under a `list` posture pages; under `none` it is `vfs.unauthenticated` at the first page (READ, `roadmap.md:167`). The spec covers anonymous only as a column to measure (§4).
- Widen the spec's two-mount fan-out criterion 9 to the roadmap's three mounts emitting one key (READ, `roadmap.md:165`).
- Keep the spec's rows-visited counts; they are stronger than the roadmap's rows-fetched criterion and are what the reference deployment's per-verb-call rule needs (INFERRED).
- `glob(observations=...)` filters rows in hand with no storage call (READ, `base.py:1362`); the spec is silent on `after`/`limit` beside it. The spec's own §3.4 rule is the nearest precedent: refuse them together with `vfs.invalid`, or page the in-hand rows by path; the converted spec states one.
- Under a partial view `ls` fetches every child with no rights term and judges in Python, and asks the road about every hidden child directory (READ, the placeholder memo `context/research/2026-11-14-a-listing-pays-a-road-check-per-hidden-child-directory.md:13`). The fill rule's statement count under a view (criterion 10, at most 7 a page) must be re-derived against that road cost and the I5 scale pins (`context/specifications/active/2026-11-14-the-rights-compile-is-the-same-at-every-world-size.md`), which pin the road's statement law.
- Coordinate unit 2 with the deletes spec before writing its index section: one schema bump should carry both if they land together, and a generation key would make the `(parent_id, deleted_at, name)` index redundant (READ, the deletes memo `:55-65`). The trash head's unbounded profile (§2.1) is also named by the deletes memo (`:28`), so the deletes spec may own it.
- The appendix A counts (3, 5, 2, 4 statements per call; 38 statements for a 10,000-row write) were taken at `ba77449` on 2026-10-24 and are not re-run here; the conversion re-executes them at its own HEAD.
- SQL Server reads run under snapshot isolation since the 2026-11-15 spec "on SQL Server a read never waits on a write" (READ, its title in `context/specifications/active/`); a page is a plain read and takes no lock, consistent with AGENTS.md's rule that a read never waits on a writer.
- Name collision to avoid: the index pass's internal cursor already has a field `next_after: int` (READ, `indexing.py:209`); it is private and need not change, but a reader of both should not confuse them.
- Shell, MCP and WASI rebase notes (167, 169, 171, 172) belong to those specs' conversions; the constant `ROWS_PER_CHUNK == LISTING_PAGE` claim depends on choice 1.

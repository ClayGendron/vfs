## 051-1. A read verb given a deadline returns within it, keeping every settled mount's rows and one `vfs.timeout` record at each unanswered mount's prefix, and a mutating verb given one is refused `vfs.invalid` before any statement

Verdict: specified-on-dev, under a design that replaces the unit's. The outcome is dev's `agents/2026-11-18-a-call-that-reaches-several-mounts-bounds-each-mount/spec.md`, third draft, reviewed "Fit to build" (READ its `review.md:7`): a read that reaches several storages waits a set time for each, returns the others' rows, reports the late one as `vfs.timeout` at the mount's path, cancels the late call, and never cuts a write by time (READ `spec.md:5-7`, `:89-91`, `:152-160`, `:176`). Landing 1 of that work, "A cancelled call leaves its storage intact", is built (READ `agents/2026-11-18-a-call-that-reaches-several-mounts-bounds-each-mount/spec-a-cancelled-call-leaves-its-storage-intact.md:1-7`; `documentation/maintainers/cancellation.md` exists; `src/vfs/storage/backends/database/engine.py:472-483` shields the wait), which is H6 criterion 3 (READ `roadmap.md:383`). Landings 2 to 4 (the timeout in listings, the timeout in searches, the mark and the probe) are not built: `add_mount` and `update_mount` take no `timeout` (READ `src/vfs/base.py:337-349`, `:472-481`), `MountListing.called` takes no `timed` keyword (READ `src/vfs/listing.py:212-214`), `gather_settled` waits on every sibling with no time (READ `src/vfs/listing.py:82-100`), and no verb produces `vfs.timeout`; the one producer is the shell, for a line whose deadline passed (READ `src/vfs/shell/host.py:295`). The spec's four "Choices to confirm" are open (READ `spec.md:449-456`). A follow-up draft for the paths the cancellation fix left, `agents/2026-11-21-a-cancelled-call-is-whole-on-every-path/spec.md`, is unreviewed and unbuilt (READ its line 3).

## 148-3. `session.join(principal)` narrows the very next call to the new subject set, re-grades nothing already surfaced, and returns the surfaced paths the joiner cannot read

Dev's spec 2 decides the case the other way: "A subject set that changes is another owner, so the conversation gets a new and empty session" (READ `agents/2026-11-22-a-session-is-stored-named-and-forked/spec.md` decision 9, `:220-234`).

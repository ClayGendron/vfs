## The author's questions, answered first

1. **Is decision 6's equivalence proven for every case the spec lists, and does the differential test pin it?** For row matching, yes; for the call's answer, no. EXECUTED and READ: the structural argument holds. `escape_glob` makes a root literal (`glob.py:122-132`), a composed path arm is the root's text followed by the anchored arm (`glob.py:348-364`), a composed name arm is `root/**/arm` canonicalized (`glob.py:367-383`), and the drafter's 24,897,600-verdict probe compares exactly that, trash side included. The meta bullet is reasoning, not probe evidence: the probe matches with `compile_filter`, which knows no marking. But the call's answer is more than row matching. Storage also judges an anonymous caller at each pattern's literal directory (`reads.py:654-676`, `backend.py:1576-1598`). Past the limit the spec judges at the root instead, so the two shapes can refuse differently (H1). The tests pin the matching only in part. Criterion 3 at one to three segments has no positive verdict for 20 pairs, and they are exactly the nested, `**`-holding, meta and nested-trash roots with two-component path arms (probe 4, L1). Criterion 4 is a sound backend differential. Criterion 5 is the right router differential, but it names a call count the modules do not hold (M2), and it runs no anonymous caller (H1).
2. **Is decision 3's switch a plan choice or a designed cap, and are two shapes justified?** INFERRED and EXECUTED: it is a plan choice. No call is refused or truncated at either side of 200, and only the statement shape changes, so AGENTS.md's no-cap rule is not touched. The limit is per entry, so one contiguous build on the loop stays at most 200 arms, about 10 ms at 53 µs an arm, however many entries a call reaches. Two shapes are justified by decision 8: past the limit a path arm's directory prefix leaves SQL, and for a few roots over a large tree that is the costly case, `src/**` under one root fetching the whole root. The tighter prefilter is measured only past the limit (2,000 × 8), so the benefit below it is inferred, but the inference is sound. Two things should be said, not changed. "Fit one storage fan chunk" is false on Oracle, 142 arms a chunk (probe 2), and with a wide `ext` set. The router's 200 restates `dialects.py`'s private `_PATTERN_ARM_CEILING` (L4). Two shapes also double the equivalence obligation. That is why H1 is a finding: it is a place where the shapes do not answer alike.
3. **Decision 4's root-independent facts beside the root OR on Postgres, SQL Server and Oracle: evidenced or inferred, and what closes it?** Inferred only, and the spec says so (decision 5). The landed text is stronger than the spec's evidence. `pattern_arm`'s docstring says one conjunct beside the fan "demotes every engine's multi-index OR to a scan" (`reads.py:511-513`). Its field study measured that only on SQLite, with `AND ext IN (…)`. Its Postgres claim is about a non-indexable *arm*, not a conjunct (field study §4). My own inference supports the spec. Every arm's fact disjunct contains a `name` term, and `name` leads no index (`rows.py:483-484`: only `uq_vfs_entries_parent_name (parent_id, name)` and `ix_vfs_entries_ext_kind`). So no engine can seek the facts in place of the path fan, and the facts become a filter above it. The residual risks are skip scans: Oracle's index skip scan, and Postgres 18's, on `(parent_id, name)`. Plan cost is the other risk, the same as today's fan. Two kinds of evidence would close it. The first is a plan read of decision 4's statement, 2,000 roots × 8 arms on the 50,000-row store with statistics, through the `db_test` skill: `EXPLAIN` on Postgres, the showplan on SQL Server, `EXPLAIN PLAN` on Oracle, each showing the path index serving the root OR and the facts as a filter. The second is a decision that the facts ride only on engines where that is measured. Either one, recorded in the spec, closes M1.
4. **Is the departure from "scope crosses only as pattern text" named and reasoned enough, and is everything those decisions protected kept?** Named: yes, decision 5, with the reason (the product). READ, kept: one call per entry, one transaction and one snapshot; root assertions on the router's probe, untouched; no root folded into another (nested roots both ride); the ext facts inside each arm (decision 4 keeps the wanted-ext membership inside every root clause); the meta lift per arm. Not kept, and not named: where an anonymous caller is judged (H1). The pattern-only seam had that property for free, because every composed pattern carried its own literal directory to `addressed_by`. The pure-OR rule is departed from by name, on SQLite evidence only (M1). The docstrings that restate the old rule are more than criterion 12 finds (L2).
5. **Is leaving SQLite's statistics-free tombstone-index seek out of this landing right?** Yes. READ: the spec measured it as today's defect (77 s for today's composed fan). On the same store the roots-only shape fell to 8.4 s, so this landing makes it no worse and reaches it less. It is not a statement-growth or correctness defect, and it needs its own pin, on which index each arm seeks, which the existing plan test does not read (`tests/storage/database/test_reads.py:502-525`). The one gap is durability. The defect is recorded only in this spec's Out line, which goes to the archive at landing (L9).

## Scale and the production posture

- SQL building: R root clauses plus A arm facts, where today it is R × A; each contiguous composed build past the switch is gone, and below it is at most 200 arms an entry (READ, EXECUTED probe 1).
- Statements: one per chunk of roots per side, the chunk from `arm_budget` over the budget left after the facts; LIKE roots never meet Oracle's 1,000-element `IN` cap, and the wanted-ext membership is chunk-aware as today; on Oracle `membership_budget` caps the binds at 1,000 before the facts are deducted, which only makes the chunk more conservative (EXECUTED probe 2, INFERRED).
- Facts: at most half the membership budget and at most the channel fan, else declined; declining only fetches more candidates (READ, `scope.py:141-178`).
- Candidates: bounded by the rows beneath the caller's roots, materialized whole as storage glob does today; named honestly with its direction in decision 8 (READ).
- Pairs, programs and the root hash set: linear in the candidates times their depth, in the arms, and in the roots (READ).
- Anonymous judgement: past the limit the judged paths fall from R × A composed addresses to R roots, which removes a product the spec does not count, but changes where the caller is judged (H1) (EXECUTED probe 3).
- Grep and glean keep one literal `pattern_arm` per admitted root on the entry-wide channel, linear but about 0.36 s at 10,000 roots at the spec's own 36 µs, so the "about 0.1 s" holds for glob only (L5) (READ, INFERRED).

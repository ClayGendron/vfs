## Findings, most severe first

Failure: a direct caller of `InMemoryStorage.write` (a public export of `vfs`, and the same seam criteria 1 and 2 call) builds `Entry(path='/eve/handbook.pdf', data=public_pdf)`, pads the call past 100,000 bytes, and swaps `entry.data = evil_pdf` from a second coroutine; the row is stored with `content_hash == H(public_pdf)` and the evil body (`size_bytes` 120,000 against a 14-byte body in the first script), and after reindex a victim who uploads the real `public_pdf` reads `'<!-- page 1 -->\nattacker planted words\n'`, which is the audit's second scenario restored (EXECUTED: `adversarial/toctou/race.py` and `adversarial/toctou/lend.py` under the scratchpad folder `code-review-measured-body`).

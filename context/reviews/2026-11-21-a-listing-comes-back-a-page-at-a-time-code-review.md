H2 (scale, blocker) A `glob` page rebuilds the whole pattern and root fan once per stream it fetches, `src/vfs/storage/backends/database/reads.py:1038`: `built.setdefault(position, wheres(position))` evaluates `wheres(position)` on every call, cached or not, so a page costs (chunks + 1) builds of every arm, which is quadratic in the patterns or roots, against the docstring at `reads.py:1028-1031`, "The fan is built once for each position a fill asks from". Failure, EXECUTED: through the public kernel, `glob("*.txt", paths=<4,000 roots>, limit=10)` built 84,000 root clauses and took 7.24 s for 10 rows, against 4,000 clauses and 0.77 s for the whole unpaged listing (`review/attack3.py`; the clock is scale evidence, not a test); on the storage, a page over 1,000, 4,000 and 10,000 literal patterns built 6,000, 84,000 and 510,000 arms, 6, 21 and 51 times the unpaged call (`review/attack1.py`), and 40,000 patterns did not finish one page inside 240 s. Closes: build the fan only on a miss (`built.get(position)` then compute and store), and a test that counts `pattern_arm` and `scope_root_arm` calls over a 10,000-pattern page and over a page past `COMPOSED_PREFILTER_LIMIT` roots, at most two builds per position.

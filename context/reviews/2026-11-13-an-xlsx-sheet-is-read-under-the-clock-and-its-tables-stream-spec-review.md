## Scale and the production posture

- The read: one step per stored row and per stored cell, no padding (EXECUTED). What it does not bound: the library's parse runs between two yields with no clock, so a single huge row element, or many non-row elements before, between or after rows, are parsed in time linear in their bytes, which the container spec bounds at 1 GiB declared (READ, `_reader.py:154-169`; L3).
- The region pass: two steps per stored cell (EXECUTED).
- The merge: at most two index entries per stored cell, inserted once. Probes per fold telescope to the growth of its bounds past its clear box (INFERRED from decision 8's rounds: each round's clear box is at least the bounds before it). Entries read add a term the spec does not bound: a group met in one round is read again in a later round whenever the clear box switches to a taller group whose rows leave it out. EXECUTED with an adversary built to do that every round: a comb of 6,400 teeth cells re-read per round gave 6.27 and 6.61 steps a cell at two and four rounds, falling to 5.35 at eight, because each new clear box must be larger than everything before it, so the bars that force a round grow geometrically. INFERRED: the worst case is of the order of cells times log cells; the most I measured on any shape, searched or built, is 6.61 steps a cell. Not unbounded, and the stepped clock caps the time in any case.
- The tables: at most the budget over three positions plus two rows (EXECUTED, criterion 8).
- Memory: one sheet's stored non-empty cells and the index, as stated.
- The revision bump: one render of every xlsx entry, once, on the next pass (L6).

## Scale and the production posture

- No SQL changes. Nothing here meets a dialect, a bind budget or an `IN` list.
- The note set and the part set grow by one entry per distinct note or part. Both are bounded by the input's own elements.
- The layout is one pass over the table's cell elements. It holds a `(source, copies)` pair per cell element, never per grid position, so memory follows the input. A row's drawn cells are at most its clamped width. A row of empty cells costs at least three characters a cell in the line the Builder charges, so the drawing stops at the budget or after one row (INFERRED from `markdown.py:202-203`).
- The paragraph and the row stop at `remaining` plus one piece. EXECUTED: under 200 characters, at most 215 characters are handed to the Builder, where 505 are handed today.
- Not bounded by this Design: the style lookup every paragraph makes, whose cost the document's styles part sets and not its text (H1).
- The re-render on landing touches every docx entry once (L4). That is a declared one-time cost, not a cap.
- No designed cap is added: no `RenderLimits` field, and nothing that limits what vfs stores.

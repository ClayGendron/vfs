### Scale

Method: I listed what grows. No SQL statement, bind list or batch-sized statement is touched, so the engine floors have nothing to judge. What grows with the corpus: `interner` and `lists` with the vocabulary, as `HEAD`'s map did; the posting streams with the postings, unchanged. What grows with one document and is now retained for the builder's life at its high-water mark: `chars` at 4 B a character of the longest body, `touched` at 4 B a distinct term of the widest body, `scratch` at one term. Per index visit, the multiplier this path rides (about 11,000,000 a day), the change removes a sort, a vector of strings and all but one allocation a chunk in the steady state (EXECUTED: 43,841 allocations over 200 already-seen chunks on `HEAD`, 241 on the tree).

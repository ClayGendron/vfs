## The question

Should the SQLite planner-statistics step leave `engine.py` for a module of its own under `storage/backends/database/`, so that `engine.py` falls under the 1,000-line ceiling and its held entry is removed? If so, what does the new module own, what does the host keep, and through what does each reach the other?

7. READ: `LAYERS` in `tests/lint/test_code_shape.py` places a module by its deepest named prefix, and `vfs.storage.backends` is a named rung. A new module under `storage/backends/database/` therefore names no rung of its own.
8. INFERRED, two shapes, and this memo chooses neither. In the first, the step is a class of its own that holds the state of item 4 and the parts of item 2; the host builds one and forwards the five public methods, or `backend.py` reaches the step through the host. In the second, the parts become module functions that take the host. Either way the import has to point one way: the host must reach the step for `close`, `_policy` and `_first_touch`, and the step needs the host's `session_factory`, `tables`, profile and dialect, so the step is handed those at construction or is typed against the host under `TYPE_CHECKING`.

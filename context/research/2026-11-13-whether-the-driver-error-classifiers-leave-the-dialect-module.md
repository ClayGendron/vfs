## The question

Should the driver-error classifiers leave `dialects.py` for a module of their own under `storage/backends/database/`, so that `dialects.py` falls by about 150 lines and its held entry falls with it, and if so, what does that module export, and does `dialects.py`'s bulk-insert path import it without a cycle?

## What is known today

- `dialects.py` holds 1,112 lines and is held at that count (READ, `tests/lint/allowlist.py`); the index-publish spec takes it to at most 1,142 (READ, that spec's decision 12).
- The classifiers are one banner group, "Retryable-error classification", at `dialects.py:915` to 1023: the constants `_SQLSTATE_GENERAL_ERROR` and `_UNWIND_DEPTH`, and `driver_origin`, `is_retryable`, `_is_retryable_alone`, `is_value_too_large`, `is_permanent_defect` and `is_insufficient_privilege`, about 110 lines; their private readers and constants sit under "Internal helpers" at `:1030` to 1069 (`_SQLSTATE_DEFECT_CLASSES`, `_SQLSTATE_LIMIT_CLASS`, `_SQLSTATE_INSUFFICIENT_PRIVILEGE`, `_MYSQL_PACKET_TOO_LARGE`, `_SQLSTATE_LENGTH`, `_sqlstate_of`, `_driver_code_of`), about 40 more (READ). The index-publish spec adds `is_unique_violation` and its five code constants to the same group.
- Outside `dialects.py` the classifiers are imported by `engine.py` alone today (`engine.py:84` to 87, READ), and by `indexing.py` once the index-publish spec lands.
- `dialects.py` itself reads `_sqlstate_of` in `_wrap_driver_error` (`dialects.py:1097` to 1102, READ), which restores `IntegrityError` for the bulk-insert path's raw driver errors, so a module of its own would export the SQLSTATE reader to `dialects.py`, and `is_retryable` takes a `DialectProfile`, so that module would name the profile type, a `TYPE_CHECKING` import (INFERRED).
- The layer map places a module under `storage/backends/database/` by its prefix (READ, the close spec's decision 4, which says the same of `engine.py`'s planner-statistics step).
